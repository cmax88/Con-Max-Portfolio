hello, my name is connor maxwell and i am a professional gis analyst and an unprofessional web developer.

i like to create clean and simple maps, diagrams, illustrations, and websites, both for myself and for my work. i have mapping experience in arcgis, qgis, and surfer, and graphic experience in illustrator, photoshop, and blender. my coding experience is in html, javascript, and python.

i graduated from slippery rock university with a b.s. in geographic information technology in december of 2025, where i learned a great deal of gis and spatial analysis techniques. my coding experience comes from a few classes i took while enrolled and my job as a student web dev at slip (short for slippery rock, btw).

i spend my free time writing and producing electronic music (mainly riddim/future riddim, if that means anything to you) under the stage name “malaka”. i’ve also been writing an ancient greek inspired dnd campaign for the last 5 years called “echoes of the gods”. who knows if it will ever be finished.
